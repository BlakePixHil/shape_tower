Baking sounds

The tower is an instrument

Each clean landing plays the next note of the biome's scale. A Perfect adds a harmony note, four in a row sparkle, and eight in a row blooms into a chord. A messy landing goes clonk and starts the climb again.

Streak 0 C major · marimba

Biome

Picking a biome plays its entry moment the first time, swaps the key and instrument, and changes the reverb. With ambience on you also hear its bed.

The soundtrack

Every biome has its own band playing a 32-bar theme at 100 BPM, in three variations. Layers join and leave on bar lines as the run changes. Pick a biome above and the new band takes over at the next bar line.
