# Shape Tower privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Shape Tower is a free game. This page says what the game keeps, what it sends and to whom. It covers the
Android and iPhone apps and the web version.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (level, XP, records, achievements, postcards), your settings, your Blockling's look, the
Daily Tower's tries and what you've bought are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app or
clearing the browser's site data removes them.

## The Daily ladder (only if you join it)

The Daily ladder is optional. If you pick a name to join it, the game sends our ladder server:

- the name you picked, your ladder portrait and your Blockling's look,
- a random player id the game made up on your device (it isn't linked to your phone, email or accounts),
- your scored Daily tries: height, score, checkpoints, and when you played.

Everyone who plays sees the names, portraits and scores on the ladder. Names go through a filter, and
players can report a name; a reported name is hidden behind a stand-in. You can delete your name and
all your Daily scores at any time, from Settings or the name card. The ladder doesn't store your IP
address or any other details about your device.

## Purchases

Purchases are optional and go through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). They handle the
payment; the game never sees your card or payment details. The game receives which product you bought
and the store's receipt for it, so it can unlock what you paid for and restore it on a new device.

## Ads

The phone apps show an occasional ad between runs (never during a run), from level 3, and never after
you buy Remove Ads. The web version shows no ads. The ads come from Google AdMob and are always
non-personalised: the game doesn't use your device's advertising ID, and it doesn't build a profile of
you. AdMob still receives the technical details it needs to show an ad, count it and stop fraud, such
as your IP address, device type and operating system. Where the law requires it (for example in the
EEA and the UK), Google's consent form asks you first. Google's own policy covers what AdMob does:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Achievements on Google Play Games and Game Center

If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), the game tells that service which
achievements you've earned. Signing in is optional, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

## What the game doesn't do

No accounts or passwords, no analytics or tracking, no location, contacts, camera or microphone, and no
selling or sharing of your information with anyone other than the services named above.
